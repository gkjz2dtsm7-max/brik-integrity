# Verified sources for Gipson 74-market comment
Checked live 2026-09-25.

No public file names the businesses that received the $2,176,503.63 RFSI reimbursements.

## 1. MDAC FY2025 annual report
https://agnet.mdac.ms.gov/agManage/uploads/3231.pdf
Proves: $6.2 million RFSI to MDAC in 2023. More than $5.8 million made available. $2,176,503.63 reimbursed FY2025 on 42 equipment and 7 infrastructure projects. LFPA $2,337,136.60 from 26 farmers and cooperatives through three food banks. SCBGP $422,993.37. FMNP $138,939 redeemed by 200 farmers at 29 markets and 2 stands, 8,337 recipients. Certified 36 markets. GAP 75 percent cap $750, 13 reimbursements.

## 2. USDA AMS Mississippi RFSI profile
https://www.ams.usda.gov/sites/default/files/media/Mississippi_State_Profile.pdf
Proves: USDA awarded MDAC $6.2 million. RFSI awarded to states May 2023. Names zero farmers markets. Describes catfish, pecan, produce, pea projects.
